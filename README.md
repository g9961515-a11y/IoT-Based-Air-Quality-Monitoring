About the Project
The IoT-Based Air Quality Monitoring System uses IoT technology and environmental sensors to monitor the quality of surrounding air. The sensors collect environmental information such as air quality, temperature, humidity, and harmful gas levels.

The collected sensor readings are processed by a microcontroller and transmitted to the KiwisIoT platform through Wi-Fi connectivity. Users can monitor real-time air quality information through an IoT dashboard.

This project demonstrates how IoT technology can help monitor air pollution and maintain a healthy environment in places such as homes, schools, colleges, offices, and industries.
